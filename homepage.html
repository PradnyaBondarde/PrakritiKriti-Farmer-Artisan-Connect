How It Works

Upload Photo of Agricultural Waste

Take a picture of your farm waste and upload it to our platform. Our system will analyze and identify it.

Get Smart Suggestions

Receive AI-powered recommendations on potential handicrafts that can be created from your waste materials.

Connect with Local Artisans/SHGs

Match with nearby artisans or Self Help Groups who can transform your waste into beautiful crafts.

Who Benefits

Farmers

  • Upload waste inventory
  • Track waste transformation
  • Earn eco-rewards
  • Reduce disposal costs

Artisans

  • Access free raw materials
  • Accept craft orders
  • Showcase your crafts
  • Grow your business

Administrators

  • Monitor system usage
  • Generate impact reports
  • Connect stakeholders
  • Expand the network
